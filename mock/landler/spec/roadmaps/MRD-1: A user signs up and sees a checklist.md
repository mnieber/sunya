## UI glossary

**Checklist**: the list on the home-page that guides the user in their next steps after signing up.

**Checklist-item card**: a card that shows details about the highlighted checklist-item.

**Organization switcher**: a drop-down on the top of the page for selecting the active organization.

# Milestones

## MS1: The user signs up

1. The user visits the sign-up route and signs up
   1. They sign-up with their email address and password
2. The user receives an activation email and clicks the link to activate their account
   1. After activating their account, they are immediately signed in
      1. If the account was already active, they are redirected to the sign-in page.
3. The user is redirected to their home-page route

## MS2: The user signs in and inspects the checklist

1. The user sign-ins and is redirected to their home-page route.
2. The home-page shows a checklist of things to do next:
   1. The items are
      1. Welcome
      2. Join the demo org
      3. Add your organization
      4. Create a project
      5. Add a plot
      6. Trigger a project-analysis
   2. The checklist-items can be highlighted.
      1. The highlighted checklist-item is also shown as a checklist-item card next to the check-list. This checklist-item card contains a short explanation
   3. The checklist-item items can be marked as completed
      1. The completed items are shown in gray but can still be selected
   4. The user can also hide the entire checklist
      1. This is explained on the Welcome checklist-card
      2. The checklist can be unhidden using a toggle in their user-settings
         1. The application shows a message (next to the toggle) to explain this
